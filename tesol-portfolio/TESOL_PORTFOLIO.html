Featured Curriculum Design

1st Grade ENL Lesson Plan: Main Topic & Supporting Details

Target Audience: 1st Grade | Model: Push-in ENL | Domain: ELA / Science Integration

Standards Aligned

NYS ELA RI.1.2 & WIDA Standards 1 & 2

Core Strategy

Visual Scaffolding & Sentence Frames

Anchor Text

Deserts by Q. M. Arnold

Lesson Objectives

  • Content: Students will identify the main topic of an informational text using visuals and environmental details.
  • Language: Students will produce academic speech using structured sentence frames: "The main topic is ___."
  • Social-Emotional: Structured Turn-and-Talk routines to foster safe risk-taking and collaborative peer interaction.

Research-Based Scaffolds (SIOP Model)

  • Think-Aloud Modeling: Making invisible reading processes visible to Entering ENL learners.
  • Visual Scaffolding: Utilizing high-quality imagery of environments (Deserts, Oceans, Forests) to build schema.
  • Culturally Responsive Extension: Home-literacy connection encouraging learners to discuss main topics using their home languages.
